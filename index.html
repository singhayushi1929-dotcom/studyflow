07 — LITTLE WAYS TO LEARN

Study smarter, gently.

Small strategies can make your study time feel more meaningful.

01 / RESET
☼

Start ridiculously small.

Choose one tiny step. Opening your notes and reading a page is a valid start.

02 / RECALL
✎

Test what you know.

Close the book and explain a concept from memory. Retrieval helps strengthen learning.

03 / REST
☾

Take real breaks.

Stretch, drink water, or step away from the screen. Rest is part of learning.

04 / REPEAT
↗

Return tomorrow.

Revisit topics over time instead of trying to do everything in one sitting.
